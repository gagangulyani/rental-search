# Intake Question Bank

Ask in batches of ~4 via `AskUserQuestion`. Options below are written to be
pasted as choices — each one teaches the user something about the market, so a
wrong guess is still informative.

Do not ask all of these. Ask what you need to stop guessing.

---

## Batch 1 — the four that change everything

**What does the budget cover?**
- Rent only — maintenance, brokerage, deposit all extra
- Rent + maintenance — society maintenance must fit inside the number
- Rent + maintenance + electricity — effective rent ceiling drops sharply
- Flexible to ~X for the right place

**Who will live there?**
- Single / bachelor — *many societies restrict bachelor tenants; large filter*
- Couple / family — *widest access*
- Sharing with flatmates — *most restricted, but splits cost*
- Single now, family joining later

**Where do you need to reach most days?**
- Work from home — *no commute anchor; opens far localities*
- Named office / area
- Varies, no fixed place — *needs interchange-adjacent location*

**Move-in timeline?**
- Within 1 month — urgent, low negotiating leverage
- 1–3 months — comfortable, can negotiate
- 3+ months — maximum leverage
- Fixed lease-end date

## Batch 2 — shape of the home

**Distance tolerance to a station or main road?**
Give options in both *minutes* and *mode* — walking, e-rickshaw/auto, own
two-wheeler. These price very differently.

**What does "open view" actually mean to you?**
- High floor, nothing directly blocking
- Facing park / green / water — *fewest options, highest premium*
- Just not staring into a neighbour's window — *cheap; a per-unit property, not per-society*

**Furnishing needed on day one?**
Unfurnished / semi (wardrobes, kitchen, AC) / fully furnished / flexible.

**Deposit you can put down?**
State the local norm in the option text (2–3 months is typical in NCR).

## Batch 3 — the filters that cause silent rejection

**Any absolute deal-breakers?** (multi-select)
Non-veg must be allowed · reliable power backup · covered/secure parking ·
no ground floor / no top floor · pets

**Vehicle?** None / two-wheeler / car / both. Changes both parking cost and how
much station distance matters.

**Lease length you'd commit to?** Flag that a longer commitment is real
negotiating currency at a tight budget.

**Open to sharing to afford a better society?** Ask even if they said they want
to live alone — in some societies a 2BHK is cheaper than a 1BHK.

## Batch 4 — the one people don't volunteer

**Why are you moving at all?**
- The stated reason (connectivity, space, cost)
- Something wrong with the current place — landlord, neighbours, noise, view
- Rent increase
- Want a better standard of living

Ask this **explicitly**, and late enough that they trust the conversation. It
frequently reveals that the entire framing so far was a proxy for something
else, and it changes what you should optimise for.

**Which localities do you already have in mind?**
Ask before recommending. If they name areas, research those too — do not
substitute your own list silently.

## Batch 5 — only if the search stalls

- Could the timeline slip? *Usually worth more than a rent negotiation.*
- Could you bridge with a short PG/co-living stay inside the target area, then
  move within it? *Gets you into resident groups — the channel brokers cannot reach.*
- What contacts do you already have, and what kind? *Brokers behave differently
  from resident friends.*
